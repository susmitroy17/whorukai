## 2026-09-12 - Keyboard Focus and Custom Cursors
**Learning:** When a site implements a custom mouse cursor (like the interactive cursor here) and disables the default pointer, keyboard navigation users are often entirely forgotten. Hiding the default cursor doesn't mean focus states shouldn't exist.
**Action:** Always ensure robust `:focus-visible` styles are explicitly defined, especially on sites heavily focused on custom mouse interactions, to maintain keyboard accessibility.
